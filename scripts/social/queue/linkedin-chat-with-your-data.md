I once got a confident, clean, wrong answer from an AI tool. Charts and all.

That is the real risk with "chat with your data": a fluent paragraph can be wrong in ways that read exactly like a correct one.

Four questions to ask before you act on any answer:

1. Was the number computed, or written by the model?
2. Can I see the query or calculation behind it?
3. How many rows is it based on?
4. Did it read all my data, or only a sample?

Then check three numbers you already know. If they match, lean on it for the questions you can't check by hand.

The full checklist: https://www.analyzeit.in/guides/chat-with-your-data
