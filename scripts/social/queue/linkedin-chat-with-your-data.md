"Chat with your data" is easy to demo and hard to trust.

A fluent paragraph can be wrong in ways that read exactly like a correct one. Before you act on any answer from an AI data tool, ask:

1. Was the number computed, or written by the model?
2. Can I see the query or calculation behind it?
3. Does it say how many rows it used, and how sure it is?
4. Did it read all my data, or only a sample?

Then spot-check three numbers you already know. If they match, lean on it for the questions you can't check by hand.

I put the full checklist in a short guide: https://www.analyzeit.in/guides/chat-with-your-data
