Most "why did sales drop?" questions are answered in the wrong order. People start at the total. The total is the last place the answer shows up.

The order that works: put the number on a time axis, decide whether it is a step, a trend or a blip, compare with the same month last year, split by segment, and check what you are counting before you act.

I wrote this up as a short guide, with the five mistakes that produce false alarms (small groups, looking at too many things, ignoring the calendar, one big customer, reading cause into a link).

Guide: https://www.analyzeit.in/guides/find-what-changed-in-your-data
