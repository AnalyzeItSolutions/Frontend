Spreadsheets are not going away, and they shouldn't.

They win when you need to see every cell, maintain a small ledger by hand, or share a model others will edit.

They slow you down on open questions like "what changed?", on messy or very large files, and on the monthly report you rebuild by hand.

My rule of thumb: if you could explain every formula to a colleague, stay in the sheet. If the question is "where should I even look?", hand that part to a tool and keep the sheet for the final check.
