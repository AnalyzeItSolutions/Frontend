Don't replace your spreadsheet. Know what to take out of it.

Stay in the sheet when you need to see every cell, keep a small ledger by hand, or share a model other people will edit.

Move the work out when:
- the question is "what changed?" and you don't know where to look
- the file is big or messy
- you rebuild the same monthly report by hand

Rule of thumb: if you could explain every formula to a colleague, stay in the sheet. If the real question is "where should I even look?", hand that part to a tool and keep the sheet for the final check.
