A number that moves is not always news. Every business metric wobbles. The skill is telling a move that is bigger than the wobble from one that is not. A short thread.
===
Mistake 1: small groups. Three orders to six orders is a "doubling" and almost certainly noise. Always ask how many records sit behind a percentage.
===
Mistake 2: looking at too many things. Check a hundred segments and a few will look odd by luck. The more you search, the stricter your bar should be.
===
Mistake 3: ignoring the calendar. A December spike that happens every December is a pattern, not news. Check earlier years first.
===
The full guide, with the other two mistakes: https://www.analyzeit.in/guides/real-change-or-random-noise
