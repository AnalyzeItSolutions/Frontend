Buffer's API cannot create threads. Build this one by hand in Buffer's composer (add each part with "+ thread").
===
Pasting a spreadsheet into a chatbot works until it doesn't. When a general chatbot is fine for your numbers, and when it isn't. A short thread.
===
Fine: explaining a method, brainstorming what to look at, a small file where you can check the result by hand.
===
Risky: big files (it may read only part of them), arithmetic that was written rather than computed, and getting different figures when you ask twice.
===
Rule: if the numbers go to a customer, an investor or a tax form, use something that computes on the full table and shows its working. Guide: https://www.analyzeit.si/guides/chatgpt-or-a-data-analysis-tool
